# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention and publishing private
information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through
the contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile. If the concern
involves that owner, use GitHub's report-abuse flow. Do not open a public issue
that identifies the people involved.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect reporters' privacy where
possible and choose a response proportionate to the behavior and its context.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. It does not replace GitHub's Terms of Service or Community Guidelines.
